Sentinel is a full-stack, production-ready email security platform that detects phishing, spear-phishing, and coordinated email attacks using only email metadata — headers, routing hops, and authentication stamps — without ever reading or storing email body content. The system continuously improves its own defenses through an adversarial self-evolution loop, eliminating the need for manual policy updates after each new attack pattern is discovered.

Section 1

Problem Statement & Motivation

Email remains the number one attack vector for cybercrime. According to the 2024 Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report, phishing is involved in 36% of all data breaches. Business Email Compromise (BEC) alone caused $2.9 billion in losses in 2023 (FBI IC3 Report). Despite decades of anti-spam and anti-phishing research, attackers continue to evade detection through three core strategies:

Problem 01

Identity Spoofing

Attackers impersonate trusted domains, executives, or vendors. Simple SPF/DKIM checks are bypassed through cousin domains (paypa1.com) and display name manipulation.

Problem 02

Infrastructure Rotation

VPNs, Tor, and residential proxies allow attackers to change their apparent origin between sends, defeating IP-reputation blacklists within hours.

Problem 03

Slow-Burn Campaigns

Coordinated attacks spread across weeks and thousands of IPs, staying below per-IP thresholds. Each individual email looks benign but the campaign is highly aggressive.

Problem 04

Static Defenses

Traditional tools require manual policy updates after each new attack pattern. Attackers probe defenses and adapt faster than security teams can respond.

Project Sentinel addresses all four problems simultaneously through its six-phase pipeline.

Section 2

Why Metadata-Only?

Core Design Principle: Sentinel never reads, stores, or processes email body content at any layer of the system. All detection, profiling, attribution, and self-healing operates exclusively on email envelope metadata.

Privacy & Compliance Benefits

ConcernBody-Scanning ToolsSentinel (Metadata-Only)
GDPR Article 5 compliance❌ Processes personal content✅ No personal content
Attorney-client privilege❌ Reads privileged content✅ Never reads content
End-to-end encrypted mail❌ Requires decryption key✅ Works on encrypted mail
MTA-level deployment⚠ Complex integration✅ Transparent proxy

Why Metadata Is Sufficient

The email envelope contains rich forensic evidence that attackers cannot easily forge without breaking email delivery entirely:

  • Received chain — The sequence of SMTP relay servers cannot be fabricated by the attacker without controlling every intermediate MTA
  • Authentication stamps — SPF, DKIM, DMARC verdicts are computed by the receiving MTA and are cryptographically or DNS-verifiable
  • Timing patterns — Date headers and per-hop timestamps leak timezone and behavioral patterns
  • IP leaks — X-Originating-IP, X-Sender-IP headers from webmail clients often expose the real sender IP even behind VPNs

Section 4–9

Core Analytical Pipelines

Phase 1 — Email Parser email_parser.py

The entry point for all emails. Parses raw RFC 5322 bytes using Python's built-in email module, then extracts every forensically relevant header field. The parser never touches MIME body parts.

CategoryFields Extracted
RoutingFull Received chain (all hops), relay IPs, timestamps per hop, hop count
IdentityFrom, To, CC, BCC, Reply-To, Return-Path (mismatch detection)
AuthenticationSPF result, DKIM result, DMARC result, ARC chain integrity
IP IntelligenceX-Originating-IP, X-Sender-IP, X-Forwarded-For
MessageMessage-ID domain, In-Reply-To, References chain
EncodingCharset, Content-Language, Content-Type, MIME version
ClientX-Mailer, User-Agent, sending client fingerprint

Phase 2 — Digital Twin Behavioral Profiling digital_twin_builder.py

A Digital Twin is a persistent behavioral model that learns what "normal" looks like for each sender identity. Every email from a legitimate sender updates the twin. Anomalies are scored 0.0–1.0.

Seven Anomaly Sub-Scores (Privacy-Preserving)

DimensionStorage MethodWeight
Sending hour (UTC)24-slot frequency histogram20%
Sending weekday7-slot frequency histogram15%
IP prefixAnonymized /24 subnet hash map25%
Send frequencyRolling 7-day z-score15%
Sender domainDomain frequency histogram10%
Charset encodingObserved charset set10%
Subject lengthMean ± std distribution5%
Privacy note: No raw email addresses, full IPs, or plaintext identifiers are stored in the Digital Twin. All data is either hashed (SHA-256), coarsened (/24 subnet), or represented as aggregate statistics.

Phase 3 — Bayesian Attribution Engine bayesian_attribution.py

Fuses 8 independent signals using Bayesian posterior updating to identify the attacker's likely geographic origin. VPN rotation defeats IP-only attribution — but Bayesian fusion of multiple signals makes evasion exponentially harder.

Posterior update rule (applied per signal):
P(country | signal) ∝ P(signal | country) × P(country)

Signals fused:
  1. X-Originating-IP geolocation
  2. Received chain relay IPs
  3. UTC offset in Date header
  4. Charset / MIME encoding (regional indicator)
  5. Domain TLD registrar geolocation
  6. Keyboard layout hint from header patterns
  7. ASN (Autonomous System) nation mapping
  8. Hop latency timing (geographic distance estimate)

Phase 4 — Multi-Agent Orchestration multi_agent.py

Four specialized AI agents run in a sequential pipeline using the CrewAI framework. Each agent has a distinct role, persona, and output format. When an OpenAI API key is present, agents use LLM reasoning. Without a key, each falls back to a deterministic rule engine producing identical output structure.

🔍
Detection Agent
Forensic Analyst
Checks 12+ forensic rules: SPF/DKIM/DMARC fails, display name spoofing, domain lookalike patterns, hop count anomalies, reply-to hijacking, attachment MIME types. Outputs detection score 0–100 and evidence list.
⚖️
Risk Scoring Agent
Decision Maker
Fuses detection score and Digital Twin anomaly score:
risk = 0.6×detection + 0.4×(anomaly×100)
Applies thresholds: LOW→ALLOW, MEDIUM→REVIEW, HIGH→ALERT, CRITICAL→QUARANTINE.
💬
Explanation Agent
Communicator
Generates 2–3 sentence plain-English explanation for end users. Zero technical jargon. Provides clear actionable guidance (e.g. "Do not click links — verify sender by phone before responding").
👾
Adversarial Agent
Red-Team Challenger
Simulates evasion attacks against current detection findings. Identifies weaknesses (timezone manipulation, IP rotation, domain spoofing). Feeds actionable patches directly into the Self-Healing Engine.

Phase 5 — Attack Graph Correlator attack_graph.py

Individual email analysis misses coordinated campaigns. Sentinel builds a NetworkX directed graph where emails, IPs, domains, and subnets are nodes. Edges represent "sent from", "belongs to", and "uses" relationships.

Node TypeDescriptionExample

domainSender or return-path domainpaypa1.com
subnet/24 anonymized prefix185.220.101.0/24

Connected components with ≥ 3 email nodes are classified as campaigns. Graph centrality algorithms identify the most critical attacker infrastructure nodes (C2 relays, shared sending domains).

Autonomously updates detection policies based on confirmed attacks and adversarial findings. No human intervention required.

Section 16

Conclusion

Project Sentinel demonstrates that metadata-only email analysis is sufficient to detect sophisticated phishing and coordinated email attacks without ever reading message content. The six-phase pipeline — parsing, behavioral profiling, Bayesian attribution, multi-agent AI detection, attack graph correlation, and autonomous self-healing — works together as a cohesive immune system that improves under attack rather than becoming stale.

The bulk evaluation on 7,446 real-world phishing emails from the EPVME dataset confirms the system's operational viability: 90.53% of emails were successfully analyzed in 461 seconds (16.1 emails/second with 20 workers), 4,644 coordinated campaigns were identified, and zero false QUARANTINE events were observed, demonstrating that the conservative threshold design prevents alert fatigue.

Key achievement: Sentinel is the first open system to combine privacy-preserving Digital Twins, Bayesian attacker attribution, multi-agent CrewAI detection, NetworkX campaign correlation, and autonomous self-healing into a single deployable FastAPI + Next.js platform — all operating without reading a single byte of email content.

The system is production-ready for small-to-medium deployments and requires only MongoDB activation for state persistence at enterprise scale. With the parser improvements and STIX export planned for the next release, Sentinel will be ready for integration into existing SOC (Security Operations Center) workflows.
