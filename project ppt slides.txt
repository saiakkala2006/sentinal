Slide 2: Problem Statement & Industry Challenges
Privacy & Compliance Dilemma:
Modern email security tools inspect plaintext email bodies, creating legal liabilities under GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA.
Unable to inspect end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) messages.
Sophisticated Attacker Evasion:
Attackers easily mask origins using VPNs, Tor, residential proxies, and lookalike domains.
Single-point indicator of compromise (IoC) lookups fail against fast-flux infrastructure.
Alert Fatigue & Manual Remediation:
SOC analysts are overwhelmed with alerts; manual policy updating takes hours or days while active campaigns spread.


Speaker Notes:
"The core dilemma in email security today is privacy versus detection efficacy. Traditional tools inspect message bodies, which breaches user privacy and fails on encrypted mail. Furthermore, attackers easily bypass static rules using VPNs and distributed campaigns, leaving SOC teams overwhelmed with manual policy patching."


Slide 3: The Proposed Solution
Zero-Payload Threat Detection: Operates strictly on RFC 5322 header metadata and routing envelopes—never reads, stores, or inspects email body content.
Behavioral Digital Twins: Maintains privacy-preserving sender profiles to detect behavioral deviations.
Multi-Signal Bayesian Attribution: Fuses 8+ metadata signals to unmask VPN-backed threat actors.
Multi-Agent AI Orchestration: 4 cooperative AI agents perform forensic analysis, risk scoring, end-user explanation, and red-team evasion simulation.
Autonomous Self-Healing: Closes the loop by auto-updating firewall and anomaly policies in real-time.


Speaker Notes:
"Sentinel introduces a six-phase autonomous pipeline. It builds privacy-preserving Digital Twins of legitimate users, correlates distributed attacks using NetworkX graph theory, uses 4 collaborative AI agents, and dynamically self-heals by rewriting its own defense policies in real-time."

Slide 5: Phase 1 & 2 — Forensic Parser & Digital Twins
Phase 1 — Forensic Parser (email_parser.py):
Extracts 30+ metadata fields: hop-by-hop Received: chains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results, ARC chains, X-Originating-IP, client User-Agents, and MIME encoding structures.
Hard privacy constraint: MIME body parts (text/plain, text/html) are explicitly dropped.
Phase 2 — Behavioral Digital Twin (digital_twin_builder.py):
Profiles 7 behavioral dimensions: Sending Hours (UTC), Weekday distribution, IP /24 subnet prefixes, Send frequency, Contact network (SHA-256 hashed), Domain patterns, and Charset transitions.
Weighted Anomaly Fusion: $$\text{Anomaly} = 0.20(\text{Hour}) + 0.15(\text{Day}) + 0.25(\text{IP}) + 0.15(\text{Freq}) + 0.10(\text{Domain}) + 0.10(\text{Charset}) + 0.05(\text{Subject})$$
Anomaly $> 0.50 \rightarrow$ Elevated scrutiny; Anomaly $> 0.70 \rightarrow$ Self-healing trigger.


Speaker Notes:
"The parser strips all content, keeping only header telemetry. The Digital Twin creates a mathematical baseline of what 'normal' behavior looks like for each sender using 7 privacy-preserving dimensions. All recipient identities are SHA-256 hashed and IPs are anonymized to /24 subnets."

Slide 6: Phase 3 — Bayesian Attacker Attribution
Defeating VPN & Proxy Evasion:
A single IP geolocation is easily bypassed with VPNs. Sentinel fuses 8 independent forensic signals.
Signals Combined:
X-Originating-IP & Relay hop IPs
UTC Timezone offset in Date: header
MIME Charset encoding distributions
Domain registrar TLD & ASNs
Inter-hop routing latency & Geographic timing
Bayesian Posterior Formulation: $$P(\text{Country} \mid \text{Signals}) \propto P(\text{Signals} \mid \text{Country}) \times P(\text{Country})$$
Result: To evade attribution, an attacker must falsify all 8 layers simultaneously rather than simply switching their proxy IP.


Speaker Notes:
"Attackers routinely hide behind VPNs. However, changing an IP doesn't automatically change their client timezone offset, MIME language character sets, or relay hop latencies. By fusing these signals using Bayesian inference, we establish persistent geographic and infrastructure attribution."

Slide 7: Phase 4 — Multi-Agent AI Orchestration
Sequential 4-Agent Architecture (powered by CrewAI / GPT-4 + deterministic fallbacks):
Detection Agent (Forensic Analyst): Evaluates SPF/DKIM/DMARC, display-name spoofing, Levenshtein lookalike domains, header injection, and suspicious relay hop counts (Score 0–100).
Risk Scoring Agent (Decision Engine): Fuses forensic score with Digital Twin anomaly score: $$\text{Risk Score} = 0.6 \times \text{Detection} + 0.4 \times (\text{Anomaly} \times 100)$$
0–35: ALLOW | 35–50: REVIEW | 50–75: ALERT | 75–100: QUARANTINE
Explanation Agent (Communicator): Generates transparent, plain-English explanations for end users and non-technical staff.
Adversarial Agent (Red-Team Challenger): Simulates bypass strategies against the current detection and feeds evasion intelligence directly to the Self-Healing engine.


Speaker Notes:
"Our multi-agent system uses specialized roles. The Detection Agent uncovers technical indicators; the Risk Agent computes a fused score; the Explanation Agent produces plain-English explanations so end users understand why an email was flagged; and the Adversarial Agent actively tries to bypass the rules to find blind spots."

Slide 8: Phase 5 & 6 — Attack Graphs & Self-Healing Defense
Phase 5 — Attack Graph Correlator (attack_graph.py):
Built on NetworkX: Models entities (email, ip, domain, subnet, msgid_domain) as a directed graph.
Discovers coordinated phishing waves across distributed senders via Degree and Betweenness Centrality.
Identifies campaigns when $\ge 3$ emails share infrastructure hubs.
Phase 6 — Autonomous Self-Healing (self_healing.py):
Eliminates human response delays through autonomous policy adaptation:
Risk $\ge 75$ (Quarantine): Auto-appends malicious IP & domain to firewall blocklists.
Adversarial feedback: Dynamically tightens anomaly thresholds and expands suspicious hour matrices.
Maintains a full, tamper-evident audit log for SOC governance.

Speaker Notes:
"Phase 5 connects individual emails into a macro-level graph to detect large-scale campaigns that distribute low-volume emails across multiple IPs. When confirmed attacks or evasion patterns are identified, Phase 6 automatically patches the defense policies in real-time with complete audit logging."

Slide 11: Privacy & Security Architecture
Strict Privacy by Design:
No email body, attachment payload, or message text is ever parsed, decrypted, or stored.
Compatible with transparent MTA-level proxies and end-to-end encrypted email systems.
Data Minimization & Anonymization:
Recipient and contact networks stored solely as SHA-256 one-way hashes.
IP addresses coarsened to /24 CIDR blocks to prevent user location tracking.
Enterprise Ready: Full compliance alignment with GDPR (Article 5 & 25), HIPAA, and CCPA.


Speaker Notes:
"Security should never come at the expense of privacy. By ensuring zero body access, hashing all contact graphs, and masking subnets, Sentinel is inherently compliant with GDPR and enterprise privacy standards."
